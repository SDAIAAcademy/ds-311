A golden set

Gold questions measure each rater against a known answer

What it is

Complaints labelled by experts and adjudicated: the expected answer is known.

How it is used

Mixed into each rater's work, unmarked. Accuracy on them estimates accuracy on the rest.

What it must cover

Every category and every language variety, including the rare ones a rater might miss.

What goes wrong. A golden set of easy, common complaints scores everyone highly and tests nothing.

Agreement (Day 1) says whether raters agree with each other; gold says whether they agree with the expected answer. Both matter: two raters can agree on the wrong label. Bayyin's gold answers come from Khulood and an expert panel.

What you can stratify on

Stratify on what is known before labelling

  • The true category is what labelling produces: not available yet.
  • The language variety is known from language detection.
  • A pre-label from keywords or a model is known, and right about four times in five.
  • 24 strata: four varieties times six pre-labels.

The smallest strata

Mixed text pre-labelled WARRANTY, English pre-labelled WARRANTY: the ones that matter most for gold.

A pre-label is imperfect, so the stratified sample's coverage is checked after experts label it (task 4 of the lab). Stratifying on a proxy is common practice in survey sampling: it raises the chance of rare cases without needing to know them.

The trade. A floor costs precision in the large strata and buys enough rare cases to test raters on them.

Proportional allocation alone would give the mixed-WARRANTY stratum (111 of 30,000) under one item. The floor of six is Bayyin's choice; a larger golden set or a higher floor are both defensible. Ask the room what floor their rarest category would need.

Gold that stays useful

Raters learn gold questions they see too often

  • Draw gold from the same mix of varieties and lengths as the work, so it cannot be spotted.
  • Rotate: each gold item appears to a rater at most a few times.
  • Keep a hidden half for audits nobody has seen.
  • Version the golden set with the guideline: a changed rule changes some gold answers.

Turning scores into a decision

Judge the lower end of the interval, after a minimum count

MIN_GOLD, LOWER = 30, 0.80

def status(row):
    if row.n < MIN_GOLD:
        return "too few"
    return "pass" if row.lo >= LOWER else "watch"

Why a minimum

9 of 9 right has an interval reaching down to about 0.70.

Why the lower bound

A rater passes when we are fairly sure they are good enough, not when they might be.

The thresholds (30 gold answers, 0.80) are Bayyin's choices and belong in the guideline's operations section. Ask: what does a rater on "watch" do tomorrow? At Bayyin, their single labels go to a re-label queue until they pass again.

What a label costs

Cost per accepted label counts every check, not only the first pass

Rater time

Seconds per label times the hourly rate, for every label made.

Review time

Audits and adjudication: 90 seconds of a more senior person.

Divide by accepted

Only labels that passed the design's own checks count.
